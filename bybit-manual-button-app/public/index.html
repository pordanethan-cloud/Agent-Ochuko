Understand the Desk Buttons

Designed for reflex-speed scalping. Every button has a dedicated structural purpose aligned with the range trading strategy.

Support Execution

BUY AT SUPPORT (LIMIT)

Places a resting Limit Buy order precisely at your configured Support level. Prevents slippage by waiting for market wicks to fill your bid at the bottom of the range.

Trigger: When price approaches support with slowing downward momentum.
Immediate Entry

BUY MARKET

Fires an immediate market buy order for your configured USDT trade size. Used when price tests support and begins aggressively bouncing upward, requiring instant fill.

Trigger: Confirmed bounce wick off support with rising volume.
Resistance Execution

SELL AT RESISTANCE (LIMIT)

Places a resting Limit Sell order at your configured Resistance level to take full profit before mean-reversion sellers step in.

Trigger: Pre-set limit when entering long at support.
Immediate Exit

SELL MARKET

Instantly liquidates your position at the best available bid. Used when resistance is swept or when sudden market conditions signal an early exit.

Trigger: Resistance reached or sudden bearish order flow shift.
Risk Reduction

CLOSE 50% (TAKE PROFIT)

Immediately sells exactly 50% of your available base token balance. Locks in profit at the Range Midpoint (equilibrium) and lets the remaining 50% ride risk-free.

Trigger: Price crosses 50% Midpoint of the range.
Hard Circuit Breaker

EMERGENCY PANIC CLOSE 100%

Two-step safety action: Immediately cancels ALL resting open orders on Bybit, then executes a 100% Market Sell of your position back to cash USDT.

Trigger: Support invalidation or unexpected black swan volatility.
